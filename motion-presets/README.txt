MOTION PRESET PREVIEWS
======================

These are short preview clips shown on the Motion page.

Drop a short muted MP4 at motion-presets/<id>.mp4 for each preset. Optionally
add a still poster image at motion-presets/<id>.jpg.

Expected preset IDs and filenames:

  push-in.mp4          - Slow push in
  pull-out.mp4         - Slow pull out
  pan-left.mp4         - Pan left
  pan-right.mp4        - Pan right
  tilt-up.mp4          - Tilt up
  tilt-down.mp4        - Tilt down
  truck-left.mp4       - Truck left
  truck-right.mp4      - Truck right
  jib-up.mp4           - Jib up
  jib-down.mp4         - Jib down
  orbit-left.mp4       - Orbit left
  orbit-right.mp4      - Orbit right
  arc-left.mp4         - Arc left
  arc-right.mp4        - Arc right
  crash-zoom-in.mp4    - Crash zoom in
  crash-zoom-out.mp4   - Crash zoom out
  dolly-zoom.mp4       - Dolly zoom
  whip-pan.mp4         - Whip pan
  handheld-follow.mp4  - Handheld follow
  tracking-follow.mp4  - Tracking follow
  bullet-time.mp4      - Bullet time
  reveal-pullback.mp4  - Reveal pullback

Files with other names are ignored by the Motion page. Clips of roughly
4-8 seconds that loop cleanly work best.
